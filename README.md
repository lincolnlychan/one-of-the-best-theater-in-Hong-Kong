# Hong Kong Disneyland has one of the best movie theater in Hong Kong. 
The theater in question is the Mickey's PhilharMagic

It has a 150 foot-wide seamless projection screen, able to project in 3D and able to have special effects including water and scents throughout the film's presentation. 

The audience can truly immense themselves into the film as the film fill up their peripheral vision. 

Its features are truly one of a kind in Hong Kong. 

The only theater in Hong Kong that is the most similar to Mickey's PhilharMagic is the CGV D2 Place ScreenX Theater which has 3 separate screens and has no special effects. 

I visit Mickey's PhilharMagic almost every time I go to Hong Kong Disneyland as no other theater in Hong Kong can match theatrical experience it provides and it has low wait times.

As it is a great theater, Disney often chose to premiere their films in Hong Kong using this theater. 

If you come to Hong Kong Disneyland, I recommend you go and watch Mickey's PhilharMagic!
